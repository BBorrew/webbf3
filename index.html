About

I build and evaluate systems that keep people in control of AI-assisted work. In a study with 12 data workers I showed that the unit of navigation in an analytical conversation changes the fidelity of what gets handed off. In TTAT I turned task templates into auditable web workflows with bounded actions and reviewable evidence, evaluated on WebArena and with 12 industrial users. I am interested in interfaces and audits that make AI assistance inspectable rather than merely convenient.

I am in the final year of the five-year integrated master's (MInf) in Informatics at the University of Edinburgh, after an exchange year at the University of California, Davis. I am applying to PhD programs starting in autumn 2027.
